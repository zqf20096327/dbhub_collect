# Tikvah Psycem  - Psychological Services & Integrated Solutions

## About Tikvah Psycem 

Tikvah Psycem  is an integrated ecosystem of psychological and professional services designed to generate measurable impact on individuals, organizations, and communities. We combine evidence-based psychology, strategic management, technology, law, finance, and social responsibility in an intervention model focused on efficiency, technical rigor, and sustainability.

## Our Services

### Mental Health, Rehabilitation & Intervention
- **Clinical, Social & Organizational Psychology** – Psychological assessment, case formulation, individualized intervention planning and continuous follow-up, focusing on mental health, trauma, resilience, and personal and professional performance.
- **Speech Therapy** – Phonetic, phonological and linguistic assessment, accompanied by rehabilitation of speech, communication and language disorders, in child development contexts and adult rehabilitation.
- **Sign Language Speech Therapy** – Specialized support for people with hearing impairments, with focus on accessible communication, linguistic mediation, school integration and adjustment of pedagogical and institutional materials.
- **Occupational Therapy** – Functional assessment and (re)habilitation of daily living activities, with focus on autonomy, social participation and adaptation of home, educational and clinical environments.
- **Psychological Crisis Support** – Protocol-based intervention in emergency, disaster, trauma, violence and critical life transition situations, with rapid response teams and support for victims and companions.
- **Individual, Family & Group Therapies** – Structured therapeutic programs based on evidence models such as CBT, systemic approaches and other psychotherapeutic modalities, with short, medium and long-term goals.

### Training, Internships, R&D & Advanced Capacity Building
- **Courses & Training** – Technical, psychological, organizational and socioeducational training programs, with focus on transfer of practical skills and integration in health, education, justice and civil society contexts.
- **Psychological First Aid (PFA)** – Training modules in initial psychological rescue, disaster response and emergency support, aimed at health, education, public safety professionals and rescue organizations.
- **Supervised Internships** – Clinical, educational and community internship programs in psychology, speech therapy, occupational therapy, mental health and social intervention, with regular supervision, internship contract and professional guidance.
- **Professional Internship Programs (PEP)** – Practical internships integrated in market context, designed in partnership with higher education institutions and health, education and social services organizations.
- **Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E)** – Development of indicator structures, logical frameworks, evaluation plans and impact reporting systems for social, health, educational and community projects.
- **Research & Development (R&D)** – Applied research in psychology, mental health, education, social policies, justice, post-disaster and intervention models in contexts of vulnerability and social change.

### Project Management, Administration & Institutional Operations
- **Complete Management of Social, Health & Institutional Projects** – Comprehensive support throughout project cycle: diagnosis, planning, resource mobilization, implementation, monitoring, evaluation and results reporting, with focus on programmatic effectiveness.
- **Administration & Operations** – Design of administrative systems, workflows, procedure manuals, document management and institutional compliance systems.
- **Management of Non-Profit Organizations & Social Projects** – Specific support in human resources management, logistics, server and partner contracting, and partnership management with international agencies and financiers.
- **Structured Volunteering** – Volunteering programs in social psychology, social assistance, clinical psychology, early childhood education, nutrition, health and therapies, with framework sheet, activity plan and impact evaluation.
- **Community Volunteering** – Post-disaster support campaigns, mental health activities, community awareness, cleaning actions, education campaigns and psychosocial support activities in partnership with local communities.

### Business Management, HR, Economics & Taxation
- **Business Management** – Organizational diagnosis, institutional strategy definition, alignment of internal processes, organizational change management and optimization of team and operational unit performance.
- **Business Management** – Strategic planning, business modeling, market objective definition, risk analysis and performance indicators for organizations in various sectors.
- **Business Consulting** – Support in conception and implementation of commercial projects, service portfolio expansion, restructuring of operational models, process reengineering and efficiency improvements.
- **Human Resources Management** – Development of recruitment and selection systems, performance evaluation, individual development plans, recognition programs and career policies.
- **Human Resources Administration** – Organizational climate management, remuneration policies, benefits, turnover, absenteeism and workplace wellness programs.
- **Human Resources Planning** – Personnel needs estimation, succession planning, competency management and alignment between talent supply and long-term organizational strategies.
- **Organizational Climate Management** – Workplace environment diagnostics, conflict resolution, stress and burnout management, strengthening of organizational culture and promotion of safety and trust cultures.
- **Applied Economic Analysis** – Impact evaluation of policies and programs, cost-benefit analysis, financial scenario modeling and support for strategic decision-making in public and private sectors.
- **Human Resources Economics** – Articulation between HR management, operational costs, productivity and performance, with focus on waste reduction and improving return on investment in human capital.

### Business, Legal, Tax & Accounting Services
- **Accounting & Auditing** – Structuring of accounting systems, preparation of financial statements, management accounting, internal audit and guarantee of transparency and regulatory compliance.
- **Corporate Taxation** – Tax planning, legal framework analysis, tax obligation management, support in debt regularization and guidance on compliance with local tax legislation.
- **Applied Jurisprudence** – Study of relevant case law, drafting of legal opinions, support in administrative and contentious proceedings, with focus on human rights, mental health, data protection and institutional responsibility.
- **Business & Institutional Advisory** – Support in governance, internal policies, risk management, organizational compliance and development of internal standards aligned with international standards.

### Technology, Innovation & Communication
- **Information Technology (IT)** – Network infrastructure management, information systems, data security, electronic document management and technical support to psychosocial, administrative and field teams.
- **Digital Solutions Development** – Patient management platforms, therapeutic sessions, beneficiaries, projects, impact indicators and monitoring and evaluation systems, with focus on security, data privacy and usability.
- **Publications & Media** – Production of technical, informative and educational content, institutional reports, training materials, awareness campaigns and organized and structured digital communication.

### Sustainability, Social Responsibility & Community
- **Organizational Sustainability** – Planning of long-term sustenance models, diversification of funding sources, human resources management and integration of social responsibility practices in the institution's mission and values.
- **Social Responsibility** – Design and implementation of social impact programs in education, health, nutrition, protection of vulnerable groups and community therapy, with systematic monitoring of results.
- **Outreach & Community Support** – Proximity interventions with communities, mobile services, awareness activities, support groups and partnerships with community leaders and local organizations.
- **Community Engagement & Volunteering** – Structured volunteering programs and one-off actions, with focus on promotion of mental health, education, basic assistance and post-disaster support, aligned to program plans and M&E systems.

### Integrated Services: 360° Intervention Model

Tikvah Psycem  integrates mental health, speech therapy (including Sign Language), occupational therapy, training, internships, volunteering, business management, business management, human resources, taxation, accounting, technology, law, social responsibility and community into a single 360° intervention model. Each intervention is designed strategically, based on rigorous diagnosis, continuous follow-up and systematic evaluation of impact, adapted to the institutional, economic and cultural context in which the organization operates.
