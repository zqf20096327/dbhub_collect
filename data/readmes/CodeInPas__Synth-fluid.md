# SYNTH-FLUID: Reactor Control

<img width="1113" height="667" alt="syth" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/adaada41-f9ec-4c7b-ac28-53040c487d13" />


**SYNTH-FLUID: Reactor Control** is an analytical sci-fi simulation game built with **Free Pascal (FPC) / Lazarus IDE**, featuring retro-futuristic visuals powered by **BGRABitmap**, real-time telemetry via **TAChart**, SQLite career persistence, and immersive soundscapes driven by **BASS Audio**.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Analytical Simulation Physics:** Balance plasma mass, thermal thresholds, magnetic containment fields, and cryogenic cooling systems.
* **Retro CRT Terminal Interface:** Execute text-based commands (`INJECT`, `COILS`, `COOL`, `DUMP_CORE`, `REPAIR COOL`) with custom word-wrap logging.
* **Auto-Calculator Dashboard:** Real-time calculation and display of optimal toroidal (`T`) and poloidal (`P`) magnetic coil targets to eliminate guesswork.
* **Emergency Anomaly System:** React quickly to sudden operational crises like coolant pipe leaks, jammed fuel injectors, and external magnetic storms.
* **Core Overload (Boss Phase):** Survive high-tension final-stretch protocol crises featuring accelerated heating, aggressive plasma drift, screen shake, and dynamic background radar electric arcs (`DrawElectricArcs`).
* **Career & Intermission Shop:** Bank your generated Net Energy across shifts and spend it on permanent Hull upgrades or max-coolant efficiency enhancements via SQLite.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Libraries

* **Language:** Object Pascal (Free Pascal / Lazarus IDE)
* **Graphics & Rendering:** BGRABitmap (Custom owner-drawn dashboard, CRT scanlines, ambient dust, particles, and radar rendering)
* **Telemetry Charts:** TAChart (`TLineSeries` for energy and thermal monitoring)
* **Database:** SQLite (`UDatabaseManager` for career/save management)
* **Audio Engine:** BASS Audio Library (`UAudioManager` for SFX and dynamic audio feedback)
