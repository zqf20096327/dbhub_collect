# 🚀 AI Career Copilot

AI Career Copilot is a full-stack web application designed to help job seekers analyze their resumes, track report histories, and get intelligent career guidance.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Backend:** Python, FastAPI
* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, Templates
* **Database:** TiDB Cloud
* **ORM:** SQLAlchemy
