# Smart-Retails_Sales_Analysis
Smart Retail Sales Analytics is an end-to-end data analytics project using SQL, TiDB Cloud, Python, Pandas, and Power BI. The project analyzes sales, revenue, customers, products, payment methods, and profitability. Interactive Power BI dashboards provide business insights and support data-driven decision-making.


# 🛒 Smart Retail Sales Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

**Smart Retail Sales Analytics** is an end-to-end data analytics project designed to analyze supermarket sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and profitability.

The project uses **SQL for data analysis and database management, Python for data exploration and preprocessing, and Power BI for interactive business dashboards**. The final solution provides actionable insights into revenue, transactions, customer segments, payment methods, product lines, and gross profitability.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall retail sales performance
* Identify top-performing product categories
* Analyze revenue across branches and cities
* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Compare member and normal customers
* Analyze payment method preferences
* Monitor gross income and gross margin
* Analyze customer ratings
* Identify sales trends over time
* Build an interactive business intelligence dashboard
