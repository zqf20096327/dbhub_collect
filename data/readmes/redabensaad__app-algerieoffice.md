# Algérie Office

B2B/B2C marketplace connecting businesses, customers, consultants and partners.

> \> [Live Preview](https://redabensaad.github.io/algerieoffice)

---

$ Context

Originally developed as a full-stack marketplace platform to centralize business visibility, commercial interactions and professional networking within a single ecosystem.

The project was designed for the Algerian market and deployed on Microsoft Azure as a production-ready business platform.

---

$ What Was Built

> \> Business directory and company profiles<br>
> \> B2B and B2C marketplace workflows<br>
> \> Customer and partner management<br>
> \> OAuth2 authentication and two-factor authentication<br>
> \> Real-time notifications with WebSockets<br>
> \> Email and SMS integrations<br>
> \> Business reporting and analytics<br>
> \> Excel import and export<br>
> \> Multi-language support<br>

$ Architecture

Monolithic architecture built with Spring Boot MVC.

The application combines marketplace workflows, authentication, business management, reporting, notifications and cloud integrations within a single system.

Security is handled through Spring Security, OAuth2 and two-factor authentication mechanisms.

---

$ Project Status

> \> Archived production version<br>
> \> Originally deployed on Microsoft Azure<br>
> \> Modernization in progress using Spring REST APIs and Angular<br>

---

$ Historical Notes

This repository preserves the original version of the platform developed between 2019 and 2020.

It is maintained as a portfolio project showcasing marketplace workflows, security implementation, cloud integrations, reporting capabilities and real-world business requirements.
