# Awesome Postgres Books

A curated list of excellent books to learn **PostgreSQL** — from beginner‑friendly introductions to advanced internals, administration, and performance tuning.

Based on the official PostgreSQL Books page and community recommendations.

---

## 🆕 Official & Modern Books

### 📘 General PostgreSQL

* [**Postgres by Example**](https://github.com/boringcollege/postgres-by-example)
  *Dariush Abbasi (2026)*
  A hands-on introduction to PostgreSQL using annotated SQL examples.
  

* [**Decode PostgreSQL: Understanding the World’s Most Powerful Open Source Database Without Writing Code**](https://decodepress.gumroad.com/l/postgresql)  
  *Ellyne Phneah (2025)*  
  A high‑level, beginner‑friendly overview of PostgreSQL without requiring SQL expertise.

* [**PostgreSQL Mistakes and How to Avoid Them**](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/PostgreSQL-Mistakes-and-How-to-Avoid-Them/Jimmy-Angelakos/9781633436879)  
  *Jimmy Angelakos (2025)*  
  Practical guidance on avoiding common PostgreSQL pitfalls.

* [**Introduction to PostgreSQL for the Data Professional**](https://www.red-gate.com/library/introduction-to-postgresql-for-the-data-professional)  
  *Ryan Booz & Grant Fritchey (2025)*  
  PostgreSQL focused on analytics, reporting, and data workflows.

* [**Mastering PostgreSQL 17**](https://www.packtpub.com/product/mastering-postgresql-17/9781836205968)  
  *Hans‑Jürgen Schönig (2024)*  
  In‑depth coverage of PostgreSQL 17 features, internals, and performance.

* [**Learn PostgreSQL – Second Edition**](https://www.packtpub.com/product/learn-postgresql-second-edition/9781837635641)  
  *Luca Ferrari & Enrico Pirozzi (2023)*  
  A comprehensive introduction to PostgreSQL fundamentals.

## ⚙️ Administration & Practical Use

* [**PostgreSQL 16 Administration Cookbook**](https://www.packtpub.com/product/postgresql-16-administration-cookbook/9781803246542)  
  *Gianni Ciolli, Boriss Mejías, Jimmy Angelakos, Vibhor Kumar & Simon Riggs (2023)*  
  Task‑oriented recipes for managing PostgreSQL 16 in production.

* [**High Performance PostgreSQL for Rails**](https://pragprog.com/titles/aapsql/high-performance-postgresql-for-rails/)  
  *Andrew Atkinson (2024)*  
  Performance tuning PostgreSQL‑backed Ruby on Rails applications.

---

## 🧠 Advanced & Niche Topics

* [**PostgreSQL 14 Internals**](https://postgrespro.com/community/books/internals)  
  *Egor Rogov (2023)*  
  Deep dive into PostgreSQL internals, storage, and execution engine.

* [**POSTGRES: The First Experience**](https://postgrespro.com/community/books/first-experience)  
  *Pavel Luzanov, Egor Rogov & Igor Levshin (2023)*  
  Conceptual foundations and design philosophy of PostgreSQL.

* [**PostgreSQL for Jobseekers**](https://www.packtpub.com/product/postgresql-for-jobseekers/9781837638130)  
  *Sonia Valeja & David Gonzalez (2023)*  
  Career‑oriented guidance for PostgreSQL professionals.

* [**PostgreSQL – Architecture et notions avancées**](https://www.dunod.com/sciences-techniques/postgresql-architecture-et-notions-avancees) *(French)*  
  *Guillaume Lelarge & Julien Rouhaud (2023)*  
  Advanced PostgreSQL architecture and internals (French language).

## 🌟 Community Favorites & Classics

### 📗 Beginner / Core PostgreSQL

* [**PostgreSQL: Up and Running**](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/postgresql-up-and/9781491963402/)  
  *Regina Obe & Leo Hsu*  
  A practical introduction to PostgreSQL usage and administration.

* [**Practical PostgreSQL**](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/practical-postgresql/9781449374030/)  
  *Joshua Drake & John Worsley*  
  A classic hands‑on PostgreSQL guide.

### 🛠️ Application Development & Performance

* [**The Art of PostgreSQL**](https://theartofpostgresql.com/)  
  *Dimitri Fontaine*  
  SQL‑centric application design and performance best practices.

---

## 📌 Notes

* Prefer **book editions matching your PostgreSQL version**.
* PostgreSQL evolves quickly — newer editions matter.
* Official documentation remains the ultimate reference; books add context and real‑world experience.
