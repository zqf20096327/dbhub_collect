ArcQuill 是一款 Windows 桌面端的英语生词本。查词走本机离线词典，接上任意 OpenAI 兼容接口后可以补中文释义、同义语差别与固定搭配。读到的词收进自己的词库，按间隔重复复习，能导出成 Anki 卡片或 CSV。数据存在本机。

ArcQuill is a Windows vocabulary notebook for English learners. Look words up in a local dictionary, optionally connect any OpenAI-compatible endpoint for Chinese glosses and sense differences, keep what you save under spaced repetition, and export to Anki or CSV. Your data stays on your machine.
